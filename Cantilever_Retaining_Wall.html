Design Steps of Cantilever Retaining Wall

Structural Engineering Design Procedure

Given Data:

Height of wall = m

Unit weight of backfill soil = kN/m²

Step 1

Computation of Initial Dimensions of the Wall

1. Depth of foundation

Df =
q₀(1 - sinɸ)² γₛ(1 + sinɸ)

Overall depth of retaining wall = Df + wall height = m = H

2. Select the initial sizes

  • Width of footing = B = 0.5H to 0.7H = m
  • Width of heel = 0.5B = m
  • Thickness of base slab = H/12 = mm
  • Adopt top thickness of stem = mm
  • Height of stem = overall depth of retaining wall − thickness of base slab
